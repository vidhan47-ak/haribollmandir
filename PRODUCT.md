# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users
Devotees and seekers of Sree Chaitanya Mahaprabhu Sree Radha Madhav Mandir (Hariboll Mandir) in Pratap Bagh, Jalandhar, Punjab, alongside newcomers discovering Gaudiya Vaishnavism for the first time. Visitors come to take darshan, keep a daily devotional rhythm of bhajan, aarti and the sacred calendar, read scriptures and bhajans in the Grantha Mandir, learn the temple's heritage and guru-parampara, check darshan timings, and plan a visit or offer seva. The experience is fully bilingual in English and हिंदी, serving both English-reading and Devanagari-first devotees with equal care.

## Product Purpose
A sacred online home for the temple, a living and always-open extension of the mandir. It exists so that anyone, anywhere, can receive darshan, deepen their bhakti each day, and connect with the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition of Sree Chaitanya Mahaprabhu as carried forward by Sree Chaitanya Gaudiya Math. Success is a devotee who returns regularly for darshan, a bhajan, or the day's tithi, and feels the temple present in daily life.

## Conversion & proof
- Primary action: return often for daily darshan and bhakti. The homepage darshan, the Daily Bhakti companion (verse, aarti, sacred calendar) and the Grantha Mandir library are the recurring draws.
- Secondary fallback: for visitors not ready to return daily, plan a visit (darshan timings, directions, contact) or explore the scriptures and bhajans.
- The line a visitor remembers after ten seconds: "You've arrived at a living, authentic sacred home, peaceful, welcoming, and rooted in the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition of Sree Chaitanya Mahaprabhu."
- Belief ladder: this is an authentic Gaudiya Vaishnava temple in a genuine parampara; therefore its darshan, teachings and bhajans are real and nourishing; therefore returning here daily deepens my own devotion and belonging. (Inferred from the site; refine as needed.)
- Proof on hand: the guru-parampara and heritage of Sree Chaitanya Gaudiya Math (Srila Bhakti Dayita Madhav Goswami Maharaj and Srila Bhakti Ballabh Tirtha Goswami Maharaj); the Grantha Mandir library including the human-verified Bhajana Gīti; real darshan timings and the Pratap Bagh, Jalandhar location; the sacred calendar of festivals.

## Brand Personality
Sacred, warm and reverent. The voice is devotional and welcoming, never commercial and never sales-driven. It should feel serene and hospitable, like being received at the temple door: unhurried, gracious and rooted in tradition. Warmth is carried through gold, maroon and cream, the lotus and peacock motifs, and gentle, respectful language in both English and हिंदी.

## Anti-references
Explicitly non-commercial; this is a temple, not a business, so nothing should read as a product, startup or storefront. Not corporate or SaaS-like: no dashboard chrome, no marketing-funnel patterns, no hero-metric template. Not flashy, garish or salesy. Not a generic website template. Avoid anything that would make a devotee feel they had landed on an app or an advertisement rather than a sacred space.

## Design Principles
Reverence first: every screen should feel like sacred space, treating the deities, verses and holy names with dignity, with typography and imagery serving devotion rather than decoration. A living daily home: reward return visits (darshan, the day's tithi, a bhajan) over one-time conversion. Authentic tradition, gently modern: honor Gaudiya Vaishnava heritage and Devanagari with genuine assets and correct sacred text, using restraint and craft rather than novelty. Welcome everyone in their language: full parity between English and हिंदी, with clarity for newcomers as much as for lifelong devotees. Calm over loud: create warmth and depth through material (gold, lotus, peacock, gentle motion), never through noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Fully bilingual (English and हिंदी) with genuine parity. High-contrast, readable body text including Devanagari, with adequate line-height so matras and conjuncts are never clipped. Full prefers-reduced-motion support, with calm crossfade or instant fallbacks for every animation. Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast and legibility.
